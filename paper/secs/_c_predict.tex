\section{Falsifiable predictions}
\label{sec:predict}

\textbf{P1: Causal addresses beat generic IDs at fixed budget.}
Setup: freeze retriever and $k{=}N$ candidates; rerank once with address-conditioned scores vs.\ generic-ID baseline on the same 51-vs-51 miss slice.
Metric is selection accuracy: fraction of queries where the reranker selects the gold candidate.
Kill: address conditioning selects identically to the baseline on the current miss slice (no accuracy gain at same $k,N$); P1 is rejected.

\textbf{P2: Bind-once/deref-many amortizes by turn 10.}
Setup: LongMemEval oracle setting~\cite{wu2024longmemeval} with a fixed session; compare one address bind plus pointer dereferences against re-retrieval every turn.
Metric is cumulative latency and retrieval calls vs.\ answer accuracy through turn 10.
Kill: no break-even by turn 10 (cumulative cost $\geq$ re-retrieve at equal accuracy); P2 is rejected.

\textbf{P3: Mutation is cheaper and less stale than RAG upsert after 100 edits.}
Setup: apply 100 sequential fact edits; compare pointer rebinding (update address table, leave tokens fixed) against RAG index upsert (rewrite/re-embed affected chunks).
Metrics are stale-answer rate on edited facts and mean ms per edit.
Kill: rebinding leaks (stale rate $\geq$ upsert) or costs $\geq$ upsert per edit; P3 is rejected.
