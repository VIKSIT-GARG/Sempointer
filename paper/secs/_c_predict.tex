\section{Falsifiable predictions}
\label{sec:predict}

\textbf{P1: Causal addresses beat generic IDs at fixed budget.}
Setup: freeze retriever, $k{=}N$ candidates; rerank address-conditioned vs.\ generic-ID baseline on 51-vs-51 miss slice.
Metric: gold-selection rate.
Kill: address conditioning matches baseline on miss slice (no gain at same $k,N$); P1 is rejected.

\textbf{P2: Bind-once/deref-many amortizes by turn 10.}
Setup: LongMemEval oracle setting~\cite{wu2024longmemeval}, fixed session; one bind plus dereferences vs.\ re-retrieval.
Metric: latency/calls vs accuracy through turn 10.
Kill: no break-even by turn 10 (cumulative cost $\geq$ re-retrieve at equal accuracy); P2 is rejected.

\textbf{P3: Mutation is cheaper and less stale than RAG upsert after 100 edits.}
Setup: 100 edits; pointer rebinding (update table, tokens fixed) vs.\ RAG upsert (rewrite/re-embed chunks).
Metric: stale-answer rate and mean ms/edit.
Kill: rebinding leaks (stale rate $\geq$ upsert) or costs $\geq$ upsert per edit; P3 is rejected.
