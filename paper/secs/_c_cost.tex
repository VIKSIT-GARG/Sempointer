\subsection{Cost crossover}
\label{sec:cost}

Standard attention materializes full keys/values per token \cite{vaswani2017attention}; PagedAttention-style paging removes fragmentation, not per-token footprint \cite{kwon2023pagedattention}. We time pointer-plus-residual break-even on FLOPs. All numerics illustrate $d=4096$ only, \emph{non-transferable} across hardware, dtype, kernel.

\paragraph{Assumptions.} A1: Fixed $N$, $m$ with $0 \le m \le N$. A2: Token costs $B$, $B_{\mathrm{res}}$ bytes satisfy $0 < k < B$ with pointer dim $k$. A3: Overhead $L_Q$ constant in $m,k$. A4: $C_{\mathrm{init}}$ paid once, amortized over $K$. A5: Unit cost $u>0$ per byte-step (FLOPs-only).

Active bytes:
\begin{equation}
L_{\mathrm{active}} = (N-m)k + mB_{\mathrm{res}} + L_Q.
\label{eq:Lactive}
\end{equation}
Baseline holds $NB+L_Q$ bytes; saving:
\begin{equation}
D = N(B-k) - m(B_{\mathrm{res}}-k) > 0.
\label{eq:viability}
\end{equation}
The $+mk$ counts width-$k$ pointer slots: $m(B-k)$ saving minus $m(B_{\mathrm{res}}-k)$ residual. Viability: $D>0$, i.e.\ $m(B_{\mathrm{res}}-k)<N(B-k)$.

With $\Delta C_{\mathrm{step}}=uD$, break-even against $C_{\mathrm{init}}$:
\begin{equation}
K^{\star} = C_{\mathrm{init}} / \Delta C_{\mathrm{step}} = C_{\mathrm{init}} / (uD).
\label{eq:Kstar}
\end{equation}
Payoff iff $M_{\mathrm{text}} \ge K^{\star}$; $C_{\mathrm{init}}/M_{\mathrm{text}}\to0$ as $M_{\mathrm{text}}\to\infty$. For $D>0$, $K^{\star}$ is $k$-monotone: $\partial K^{\star}/\partial m>0$ and $\partial K^{\star}/\partial k>0$, since
\begin{equation}
\partial K^{\star}/\partial m = C_{\mathrm{init}}(B_{\mathrm{res}}-k)/(uD^2), \quad \partial K^{\star}/\partial k = C_{\mathrm{init}}(N-m)/(uD^2).
\label{eq:monotone}
\end{equation}
More pointer tokens/dimensions shrink $D$, delaying break-even; cheapest viable design minimizes $m,k$ at fixed accuracy.

Latency: prefill tracks FLOPs, so $K^{\star}$ approximates prefill break-even; decode tracks bandwidth, paging, fusion. FLOPs-only $K^{\star}$ is necessary but insufficient: $D>0$ with $M_{\mathrm{text}}>K^{\star}$ can still lose to gather/page-table overhead. Every $d=4096$ number illustrates analytic form, non-transferable, not a performance claim.

\paragraph{Design rationale (not a theorem).} The compactness--fidelity tradeoff has the shape of a rate--distortion problem: pointer length is the rate, task-relevant information loss is the distortion. Optimal black-box prompt compressors sit on a distortion-rate frontier far below current methods \cite{nagle2024fundamental}. We borrow only the vocabulary: it tells us to report fidelity at fixed rates (our $k$-sweep prediction) rather than single-point compression ratios, and to expect a saturation knee where extra pointer tokens stop buying fidelity. No optimality claim about our construction follows.
